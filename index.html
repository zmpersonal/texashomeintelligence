Frequently Asked Questions

What is a QuoteReady Project Brief?

A standardized summary of your home project — the problem, property context, priorities, and questions a contractor should answer — built from what you tell us.

Is QuoteReady really free?

Yes. You can create your Project Brief for free and use it with any contractor.

Can I use the brief with my own contractor?

Yes. Your Project Brief is yours to use with anyone — a contractor you already trust, one you find yourself, or one Texas Home Intelligence introduces you to.

Does Texas Home Intelligence diagnose my home?

No. It organizes the information you provide and identifies questions or items a qualified professional may need to evaluate. It does not replace an inspection or professional diagnosis.

Do I have to talk to contractors through Texas Home Intelligence?

No. Contractor introductions through Texas Home Intelligence are optional and never required to use your brief.

How many contractor quotes should I get?

For most non-emergency projects, two or three is common practice. It matters more that each contractor is quoting the same scope of work than exactly how many quotes you collect.

Where does Texas Home Intelligence get its data?

From public sources including NWS/NOAA weather data, municipal permit systems, TWDB drought data, and other government and utility sources. See our data catalog for the full list.

How often is Texas Home Intelligence data updated?

Update cadence depends on the source and is stated on each data page once a live feed is connected. Figures currently marked SAMPLE are placeholders, not live data yet — see our methodology.
