Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuoteReady really free?

Yes. You can create your Project Brief for free and use it with any contractor.

Does QuoteReady diagnose what is wrong with my home?

No. It organizes the information you provide and identifies questions or items a qualified professional may need to evaluate. It does not replace an inspection or professional diagnosis.

Do I have to use a Texas Home Intelligence contractor?

No. Your Project Brief is yours to use with anyone. If you want help finding contractors, introductions through Texas Home Intelligence are optional.
