## 1. Grounding

### The footer as it stood

Four columns: a brand blurb, then Locations, Services, Company.

| column | links |
|---|---|
| *(blurb)* | — |
| Locations | `/austin/`, `/san-antonio/` |
| Services | the seven `/austin/<service>/` pages: roofing, hvac, plumbing, fire-damage-restoration, mold-remediation, electrical, tree-trimming |
| Company | `/data/`, `/analysis/`, `/tools/`, `/methodology/`, `/home/sign-in/`, `/privacy/`, `/dashboard/` |

**D2a — the seven service links exist, confirmed.** They are rendered from the `services`
content collection in a fixed order, all pointing at the Austin page of each service. They are
kept, in their own column, in that same order.

### Inbound links, with and without the footer

Measured over all 272 built pages, counting a link as inbound if it appears anywhere in the
served HTML (scripts stripped). "Outside its own subtree" is the same rule `check-orphans` uses.

| page | inbound, all | inbound, non-footer | non-footer, outside its own subtree |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/austin/roofing/` and the other six Austin service pages | 272 | 4 | 4 — `/austin/`, `/`, the San Antonio twin, `/services/` |
| the seven San Antonio service pages | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| **`/tools/`** | **272** | **3** | **0** |

So the footer is not what keeps the service pages reachable: drop the footer and each still has
four inbound sources, none of them chrome. `/tools/` is the opposite and the finding worth
carrying forward — **its only three non-footer inbound links are the breadcrumbs of its own
three children.** Remove the footer link and it is an indexed orphan again, exactly as Round 29
found it. D2b keeps it, which is the right call for a reason that is measured rather than
stylistic.

### Anchor targets on `/data/` — did not exist

`/data/` renders a "By location" section whose cards are generated by a loop over the
registry's locations. The cards carried **no `id`**, so `#austin` and `#san-antonio` had nothing
to land on. Adding them is an attribute on the element the loop already emits — `id={loc.id}` —
so it touches no copy and cannot name a location the page does not list. Austin, San Antonio
and Texas all get one.

The brief asked for dead space at or under ~50% of the column's current height at 1366px —

the height the row already has, and the Connect block occupies what the blurb does not. The
row height is unchanged on desktop (225px → 226px), so the footer did not get taller to
achieve it. `1.5fr 1fr 1fr 1fr` replaces `2fr 1fr 1fr 1fr`; nothing else in the grid moved.

**Tap targets.** Before, every one of the 16 footer links was **18px** tall at 390px. After,
**19/19 are at least 44px** at both 390px and 360px, and the icon is 44×44. This is the one
change with a visible cost: the mobile footer grid is **753px** tall where it was 517px. It is
confined to phones — on a pointer device the rows stay at 18px with 25px between them, which
keeps the desktop footer the height it was.

**No Tailwind token leaked.** The sitewide stylesheet's hash changed, as it must when
`global.css` changes, so the check is the rule-level diff rather than the hash: **10 rules
added, 1 replaced, nothing else** — every one of them a `.footer-*` selector written in this
round. No utility appeared.

## 4. Two things the verification caught in itself

Both were defects in the new assertions, not in the footer, and both are recorded because the
first version of each **passed for the wrong reason**.

**The anchor raced its own page.** `/data/#austin` measured as landing 5px behind the sticky
header while `/data/#san-antonio` measured as landing correctly — with identical markup and
the same `scroll-margin-top`. The difference was the navigation: the second anchor was reached
by changing the fragment on an already-open page, which is a same-document jump against
settled layout and lands correctly whatever the margin is. On a **cold** load the browser makes
the jump and the page then shifts up ~32px as the web fonts swap in, and does not re-adjust.
So the margin is 128px, not 96px, and the replay now opens a fresh context per anchor. Both
land at 96px clear of a header whose bottom is at 69px.

**The icon was "not found" twice, for two different reasons.** First, an icon link's
`innerText` is a whitespace string — truthy — so `innerText || ariaLabel` returned the
whitespace and never read the label. Then, with the label read, it was truncated to 28
characters for the log, which cuts the word "Facebook" off the end of "Texas Home Intelligence
on Facebook". The replay now finds the icon by the class the stylesheet keys its 44px box off.

## 5. Replay changes, exactly

`footerchrome.mjs` went from 63 assertions to **89**. **No existing assertion changed its
expectation** — the seven Austin service links, the `/tools/` hub link, the absence of
`/services/` and `/start/`, the absence of "Project Brief" / "All services" text, and one
identical footer across the eight sampled pages all still hold unmodified, which is the point
of leaving them alone through a restructure. Only the file's header comment was edited, to say
so.

What is new, and why: the old file asserted a handful of links and never the **shape**, so a
column could have been renamed, reordered or emptied without an assertion moving. Added:

- the four columns and their order, and each column's links **in order** (24 assertions);
- the Connect label present, no Locations column surviving, no About link;
- the social profile: exactly one, the exact URL, `rel` naming `me` and `noopener`, an
  accessible name, an inline `<svg>` hidden from the accessibility tree and no `<img>`, no
  YouTube or Pinterest, **no placeholder or `#` href anywhere in the footer**, and no
  third-party script or stylesheet on the page;
- `/data/#austin` and `/data/#san-antonio` resolving to real elements that land clear of the
  sticky header on a cold load — the one internal link `check-links` cannot judge, because it
  normalizes the fragment away and `/data/#nothing-here` passes as `/data/`;
- every footer target at least 44px tall at 390px and 360px, and the icon 44×44;
- the footer identical with scripting off.
