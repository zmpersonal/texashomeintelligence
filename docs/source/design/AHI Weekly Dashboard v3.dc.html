DESIGN NOTES · NOT PRODUCT UI Hide via Tweaks · switch Week (heat / storm / calm) and Arrival (shared link) there too — the layout is a priority engine, and the scenarios prove it.

All four traffic sources (AI, search, Facebook, shared links) arrive differently, but every second visit asks the same question: what's happening with my home now? So the page is a fixed answer stack — is it OK → what changed → does it matter → how do I compare — and a priority engine decides what fills each slot each week. Signal scores here are computed from the visible weights, never hand-set.

1 · 5-second hero. Score + band + Δ + a plain-words verdict. The Δ chip is the return hook; the verdict kills fake urgency.
2 · What changed shares the hero row. It is the literal reason to reopen weekly. Stable weeks say so honestly — and celebrate streaks.
3 · Signals ranked by movement. Moved ≥6 points or elevated → full card. Slow signals rest in a quiet row instead of burning weekly real estate.
4 · One alert, max. The P1–P4 engine picks one; calm weeks show none. Scarcity keeps it credible.
5 · Compare is the share hook. The percentile is the social currency — it fronts the card, not the raw score.
6 · Email as the trigger. "Don't check every day" converts because it promises fewer, better sessions.
7 · Storm takeover, with decay. On a hail week storm leads everything, and the copy says the reading will ease.
8 · Empowerment framing. Calm weeks compress the whole page and become the streak card — not padded anxiety.
