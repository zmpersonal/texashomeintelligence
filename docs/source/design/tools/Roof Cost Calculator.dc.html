The short answer

A full asphalt shingle re-roof in Austin runs about $11,600 – $17,400 for a typical 22-square, single-story house — roughly $530 to $790 a square. Steep pitch, a second story, or a second layer to tear off each move the figure up by a predictable amount rather than by a contractor’s discretion.
