Share card system

One ZIP's reading, packaged as a data object

The percentile fronts every card — it is the social currency and reads in under two seconds. The score, band, and delta sit beneath it, and the footer makes the card self-branding for a stranger who sees only the image. Nothing home-specific ever appears here: no to-dos, filters, reminders, addresses, or names.

Backend-populatable. Score, band, percentile, delta, location, date, and verdict are all variable-length: the hook wraps to three lines, the score cell is fixed-width mono, and nothing is positioned absolutely against a text edge.
Amber spends itself once. The percentile figure is the only accent on the card — roughly 2% of the surface. Status color appears only in the ring and one small band chip, brightened a step for dark ground.
Thumbnail-safe. Nothing below 26px at export scale; the hook and score survive platform recompression, and the ring reads as a shape before the number is legible.
Swappable mark. The lockup comes from the design system's Logo component, so replacing the unfinished mark is a one-file change and every card format picks it up.

Cards are drawn at full export resolution and shown scaled to fit this page — 9:16 is 1080×1920, square is 1080×1080, link preview is 1200×630. Switch the ZIP in Tweaks.
